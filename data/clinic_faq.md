## Our Services

We offer general physician consultations and a full diagnostics lab under one roof.

### Consultations
- **General Physician (OPD)** — walk-in or appointment consultation for fever, cough, cold, headache, acidity, diabetes, hypertension, and other routine complaints.
- **Specialist Consultations** (by appointment) — available for cardiology, endocrinology, and dermatology on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday afternoons.

### Diagnostics
- **Blood Tests** — full blood count, HbA1c, fasting blood sugar, lipid profile, liver function tests, kidney function tests, thyroid profile, and vitamin D/B12.
- **Ultrasound** — abdomen, obstetrics (pregnancy), thyroid, and scrotal ultrasound.
- **ECG** — 12-lead electrocardiogram, available same day.
- **X-Ray** — chest, abdomen, and orthopaedic views.

## Pre-Test Instructions

### Blood Tests (Fasting Required)
- Do not eat or drink anything (except plain water) for **8 to 12 hours** before the test.
- Bring your previous reports if you have them.
- Take your regular morning medicines with a sip of water; inform the technician if you are on diabetes or blood-thinning medication.

### Ultrasound (Full Bladder Required)
- Drink **3 to 4 glasses of water** 1 hour before the appointment and do not empty your bladder until after the scan.
- Do not eat for 4 to 6 hours before the test.
- For abdominal ultrasound, avoid gas-forming foods (beans, cabbage, broccoli) the evening before.
